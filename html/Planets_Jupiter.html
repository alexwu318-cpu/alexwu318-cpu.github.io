Planet 05

Jupiter

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and the largest planet in our solar system. This enormous gas giant is known for its colorful cloud bands, powerful storms, intense magnetic field, and the Great Red Spot, a storm larger than Earth.

Facts & Statistics

Discover Jupiter

Jupiter is a massive world composed mainly of hydrogen and helium, with rapid rotation, powerful storms, faint rings, and a large family of moons.

139,820 km

Diameter

Jupiter has an equatorial diameter of approximately 139,820 kilometres.

4.6 B

Years Old

Jupiter formed about 4.6 billion years ago alongside the rest of the solar system.

24.79

Gravity

Jupiter's cloud-top gravity is approximately 24.79 m/s², more than twice Earth's gravity.

11.86 Years

Orbital Period

Jupiter takes about 11.86 Earth years to complete one orbit around the Sun.

9h 56m

Day Length

Jupiter completes one rotation in just under ten hours, giving it the shortest day of any planet.

318×

Earth's Mass

Jupiter has approximately 318 times the mass of Earth and contains more mass than all the other planets combined.
