Planet 06

Saturn

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest planet in our solar system. This pale gas giant is best known for its spectacular ring system, which is made from countless pieces of ice, rock, and dust.

Explore More ↓

Facts & Statistics

Discover Saturn

Saturn is a rapidly rotating gas giant with a low density, strong winds, a complex atmosphere, and the most extensive ring system of any planet in our solar system.

116,460 km

Diameter

Saturn has an equatorial diameter of approximately 116,460 kilometres.

4.5 B

Years Old

Saturn formed around 4.5 billion years ago during the early development of the solar system.

10.44

Gravity

Saturn's cloud-top gravity is approximately 10.44 m/s², slightly stronger than Earth's.

29.5 Years

Orbital Period

Saturn takes about 29.5 Earth years to complete one orbit around the Sun.

10h 42m

Day Length

Saturn rotates rapidly, completing one day in roughly 10 hours and 42 minutes.

0.69 g/cm³

Density

Saturn is the least dense planet and is less dense than liquid water.
