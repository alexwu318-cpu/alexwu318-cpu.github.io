Planet 01

Mercury

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and the smallest planet in our solar system. Its rocky, heavily cratered surface experiences extreme temperature changes because the planet has almost no atmosphere to retain heat.

Facts & Statistics

Discover Mercury

Mercury is a dense, rocky world with a large metallic core, a heavily cratered surface, extreme temperatures, and the fastest orbit around the Sun.

4,879 km

Diameter

Mercury has a diameter of approximately 4,879 kilometres, making it the smallest planet in the solar system.

4.5 B

Years Old

Mercury formed approximately 4.5 billion years ago during the early formation of the solar system.

3.7

Gravity

Mercury's surface gravity is approximately 3.7 m/s², about 38% of Earth's gravity.

88 Days

Orbital Period

Mercury completes one orbit around the Sun in only 88 Earth days.

0

Moons

Mercury has no natural moons and no ring system.

430°C

Daytime Heat

Daytime temperatures near Mercury's equator can reach approximately 430°C.
