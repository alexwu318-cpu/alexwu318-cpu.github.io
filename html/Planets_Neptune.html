Planet 08

Neptune

Neptune is the eighth and most distant planet from the Sun. This deep blue ice giant is famous for its supersonic winds, dynamic storms, and frigid atmosphere composed primarily of hydrogen, helium, and methane.

Facts & Statistics

Discover Neptune

Neptune is the farthest known major planet in our solar system and is one of two ice giants, featuring the strongest winds ever recorded on a planet.

49,244 km

Diameter

Neptune has an equatorial diameter of approximately 49,244 kilometres.

4.5 B

Years Old

Neptune formed roughly 4.5 billion years ago during the formation of the solar system.

11.15

Gravity

Neptune's gravity is approximately 11.15 m/s², slightly stronger than Earth's.

165 Years

Orbital Period

Neptune takes about 165 Earth years to complete one orbit around the Sun.

14

Known Moons

Neptune has 14 known moons, with Triton being the largest.

2,100 km/h

Wind Speed

Neptune experiences the fastest planetary winds in the solar system, reaching speeds of over 2,100 km/h.
