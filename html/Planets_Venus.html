Planet 02

Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and is similar to Earth in size and structure. Beneath its thick clouds lies a rocky world with crushing atmospheric pressure, volcanic landscapes, and the hottest surface of any planet in the solar system.

Facts & Statistics

Discover Venus

Venus is a rocky planet with a dense carbon-dioxide atmosphere, sulfuric-acid clouds, extreme heat, and a slow retrograde rotation.

12,104 km

Diameter

Venus has a diameter of approximately 12,104 kilometres, making it slightly smaller than Earth.

4.5 B

Years Old

Venus formed around 4.5 billion years ago during the early development of the solar system.

8.87

Gravity

Venus has a surface gravity of approximately 8.87 m/s², about 90% of Earth's gravity.

225 Days

Orbital Period

Venus takes approximately 225 Earth days to complete one orbit around the Sun.

243 Days

Rotation Period

Venus rotates extremely slowly, taking about 243 Earth days to complete one turn.

465°C

Surface Temperature

Venus has an average surface temperature of roughly 465°C, making it the hottest planet in the solar system.
