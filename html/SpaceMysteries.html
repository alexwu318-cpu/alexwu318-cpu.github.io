Case File 01

Dark Energy

Astronomers discovered that the universe is expanding faster over time, but no one knows what force is causing this acceleration. The mysterious phenomenon has been named dark energy , and it may make up nearly 70% of the entire universe.

Year: 1933

Case File 02

Dark Matter

While observing galaxies, astronomers found that visible matter alone could not explain how fast they rotate. An invisible substance known as dark matter appears to provide the missing gravity, yet it has never been directly detected despite decades of research.

Year: 1977

Case File 03

Wow! Signal

On 15 August 1977, astronomers detected a powerful radio signal from deep space that lasted just 72 seconds. The signal matched many characteristics expected from an extraterrestrial transmission, yet it has never been detected again. Its true origin remains unresolved.

Year: 1986

Case File 04

Great Attractor

Millions of galaxies, including the Milky Way, are moving toward a region known as the Great Attractor . Dense stars and dust obscure the region, making its complete structure difficult to observe.

Year: 2017

Case File 05

Oumuamua

In 2017, astronomers discovered Oumuamua , the first known object to enter the Solar System from interstellar space. Its unusual shape, acceleration, and lack of a visible comet tail continue to attract debate.

Year: 1950

Case File 06

Fermi Paradox

In 1950, physicist Enrico Fermi posed the question: “Where is everybody?” Billions of potentially habitable worlds may exist, yet humanity has found no convincing evidence of another intelligent civilization.
