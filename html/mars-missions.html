1997

Sojourner & Pathfinder

The tiny pioneer that proved mobile robotics on another world.

Sojourner was the first wheeled vehicle to operate on Mars. Weighing just 23 pounds, it used a unique rocker-bogie suspension system and relied on base station communications to navigate the rocky Ares Vallis terrain.

  • Distance Traveled: 100 meters
  • Mission Duration: 85 days
2004

Spirit & Opportunity

The robotic geologists designed for a 90-day sprint that lasted years.

Twin rovers landing on opposite sides of Mars tasked with searching for past water activity. Opportunity shattered all expectations, surviving dust storms and harsh winters to operate for nearly 15 years.

  • Distance Traveled: 45.16 km (Opportunity)
  • Key Discovery: Direct evidence of ancient liquid water
2012

Curiosity (MSL)

The nuclear-powered SUV probing Gale Crater for organic building blocks.

Using a revolutionary sky-crane landing maneuver, Curiosity touched down inside Gale Crater. Armed with a laser-firing spectrometer (ChemCam), it drills into Martian rock layers to analyze planetary chemistry.

  • Power Source: Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generator
  • Status: Active & Exploring Mount Sharp
2021

Perseverance & Ingenuity

Searching for ancient microbial biosignatures and caching samples for Earth return.

Landing in Jezero Crater—an ancient lakebed—Perseverance carries a suite of high-tech instruments and a sample-caching system. It also deployed Ingenuity, the first powered aircraft to fly on another planet.

  • Primary Goal: Astrobiology & Sample Caching
  • Companion: Ingenuity Helicopter

Ares Vallis (Sojourner)

Landed on July 4, 1997. Chosen as the landing site because it presented a wide variety of rocks washed down by ancient floods, offering a diverse geological sampling area.

  • Landing Year: 1997
  • Terrain: Rocky Floodplain

Meridiani Planum (Opportunity)

Landed in January 2004. Explored flat basaltic plains and impact craters, famously discovering hematite spheres ("blueberries") that formed in ancient liquid water.

  • Landing Year: 2004
  • Terrain: Flat Plains & Craters

Gale Crater (Curiosity)

Landed in August 2012 via sky-crane. Currently exploring the base of Mount Sharp to investigate ancient climate shifts and habitable lake environments.

  • Landing Year: 2012
  • Terrain: Impact Crater & Mountain Base

Jezero Crater (Perseverance)

Landed in February 2021. An ancient river delta environment rich in clay minerals, perfectly suited for hunting signs of ancient microbial biosignatures.

  • Landing Year: 2021
  • Terrain: Ancient River Delta
