Possible Pathways

The Road Ahead

Future exploration will depend on scientific discovery, international cooperation, responsible technology and the ability to live beyond Earth for longer periods.

01

2026–2035

Build and Test

Develop reusable spacecraft, test lunar systems, improve surface power and prepare technologies for longer missions.

02

2035–2045

Establish and Expand

Build permanent lunar research stations, develop local resources and expand scientific activity around the Moon.

03

2045–2060

Prepare for Mars

Test closed-loop habitats, radiation protection, autonomous cargo systems and technologies for long-distance travel.

04

2060–2080

Connect New Worlds

Create an interplanetary transport network connecting Earth, orbital stations, the Moon and possible settlements on Mars.

05

2080+

Beyond the Solar System

Send advanced robotic probes farther into space and continue the search for habitable environments and signs of life.
