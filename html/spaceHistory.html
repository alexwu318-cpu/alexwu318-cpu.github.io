In-Depth Story

The Apollo 11 Mission

July 16–24, 1969

Apollo 11 fulfilled the goal of landing humans on the Moon and returning them safely to Earth. The mission became one of the most important achievements in exploration history.

○ First crewed landing on the Moon

○ First human footsteps on another world

○ 21.5 kilograms of lunar samples returned

○ Global television audience followed the mission
