Planet 07

Uranus

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun and one of the two ice giants in our solar system. Its pale blue-green color comes from methane in its atmosphere, and its extreme axial tilt causes the planet to rotate almost completely on its side.

Facts & Statistics

Discover Uranus

Uranus is a cold, distant ice giant with a methane-rich atmosphere, faint rings, powerful winds, and one of the most unusual rotations of any planet.

50,724 km

Diameter

Uranus has an equatorial diameter of approximately 50,724 kilometres.

4.5 B

Years Old

Uranus formed around 4.5 billion years ago during the early development of the solar system.

8.69

Gravity

Uranus has a gravitational acceleration of approximately 8.69 m/s² near its cloud tops.

84 Years

Orbital Period

Uranus takes about 84 Earth years to complete one orbit around the Sun.

17h 14m

Day Length

Uranus completes one rotation in roughly 17 hours and 14 minutes.

98°

Axial Tilt

Uranus has an axial tilt of about 98 degrees, causing it to rotate almost completely on its side.
