Planet 04

Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is commonly known as the Red Planet because of the iron-rich minerals covering its surface. It is a cold, rocky world with enormous volcanoes, deep canyons, polar ice caps, and evidence that liquid water once flowed across its surface.

Facts & Statistics

Discover Mars

Mars is a dry and rocky planet with a thin atmosphere, two small moons, and some of the largest geological features in the solar system.

6,779 km

Diameter

Mars has an average diameter of approximately 6,779 kilometres.

4.6 B

Years Old

Mars formed approximately 4.6 billion years ago during the early development of the solar system.

3.71

Gravity

Surface gravity on Mars is approximately 3.71 m/s², around 38% of Earth's gravity.

687 Days

Orbital Period

Mars takes about 687 Earth days to complete one orbit around the Sun.

2

Moons

Mars has two small natural satellites named Phobos and Deimos.

95%

Carbon Dioxide

Approximately 95% of the Martian atmosphere is composed of carbon dioxide.
