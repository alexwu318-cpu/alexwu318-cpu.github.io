2021

James Webb Mirror

The James Webb Space Telescope features an 18-segment, gold-coated mirror measuring 6.5 metres in diameter, making it the largest telescope ever launched into space.

  • Largest telescope ever launched
  • Infrared observations
  • Looks over 13 billion years into the past
1998

Ion Propulsion

Ion propulsion uses electrically charged particles to produce extremely efficient thrust, allowing spacecraft to travel farther while using significantly less fuel.

  • 10× more fuel efficient
  • Ideal for deep-space travel
  • Used by NASA's Deep Space 1 mission
2021

Ingenuity Helicopter

Ingenuity became the first aircraft to achieve powered, controlled flight on another planet, opening a new era of aerial planetary exploration.

  • 72 successful Mars flights
  • Travelled with Perseverance Rover
  • Proved flight on Mars is possible
2023

Laser Communications

Laser communication systems use infrared light instead of radio waves, allowing spacecraft to transmit enormous amounts of scientific data back to Earth.

  • Up to 100× faster than radio
  • Higher bandwidth transmission
  • Future of deep-space communications
